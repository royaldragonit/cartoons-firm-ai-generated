## 5. Motion direction and hierarchy

Use this priority order:

1. Ah Lim's subtle, slow, shallow breathing: a small natural rise and fall in the upper chest
   or blanket, with no dramatic effort.
2. A very slight, slow push-in that remains almost imperceptible and never changes the medium
   composition.
3. Optional tiny settling in an already visible staff posture or fabric edge.
4. Optional extremely subtle ambient life only if it does not distract from Ah Lim.

Keep all motion localized and restrained. The staff should not independently gesture, reach,
turn, enter, leave, or exchange looks. In particular, keep the viewer-left overlapping arms and
hands nearly frozen. Do not animate all five staff at once.

Do not create a deterministic still-image zoom with no temporal life; the breathing should be
gently perceptible. Do not create a strong zoom, pan, tilt, orbit, camera shake, rack focus,
aggressive parallax, or looping motion.
