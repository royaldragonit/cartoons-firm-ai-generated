## Continuity and character appearance notes

The classroom desks, whiteboard, windows, school uniforms, and green trousers recur throughout. Lighting is generally flat daylight/neutral indoor light; the story moves from hospital interior to classroom, field, gate, and canteen. The black-haired student is usually in a white short-sleeved collared shirt and green trousers; the light-haired guide has very short pale hair, the same shirt, and green trousers. Several classmates are only shown in vignette form and should remain visually distinct rather than merged.
